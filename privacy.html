Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

TL;DR: Audio Tab Finder doesn't collect, store, or transmit any of your data. Period.

What We Collect

Nothing. Literally nothing. We don't collect any data from you.

How the Extension Works

Audio Tab Finder uses Chrome's built-in tabs API to check which tabs are currently playing audio. This happens entirely on your device. The extension:

  • Reads the "audible" status of your tabs (true/false)
  • Reads basic tab information (title, URL, favicon) to display in the popup
  • That's it. Nothing else.

What We Don't Do

  • We don't track your browsing history
  • We don't collect personal information
  • We don't send data to any servers
  • We don't use analytics or tracking tools
  • We don't store anything outside your browser
  • We don't sell data (because we don't have any)

Permissions Explained

The extension requests the "tabs" permission. This is required to:

  • Check if tabs are playing audio
  • Get tab titles and URLs to show in the list
  • Switch to tabs when you click on them
  • Close tabs when you request it

We use the minimum permissions necessary for the extension to function.

Third Parties

We don't share any data with third parties because we don't have any data to share. The extension works 100% locally on your device.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever change this policy (spoiler: we won't, because privacy is important), we'll update this page with a new date.
